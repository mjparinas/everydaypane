# iCloud Photos on Everydaypane

Everydaypane does **not** sign in to Apple on the server. Use a **published shared album** or copy photos into the library:

1. On iPhone or Mac, open Photos, select an album, share it, and turn on **Public Website** (or copy individual image links).
2. In **Settings › Photos**, add an **iCloud published album links** source and paste those `https://` addresses (one per line).
3. Or upload / pick the same photos into a **library** album.

For more stable links, prefer copying into Everydaypane’s photo library or Immich/Dropbox/OneDrive.
